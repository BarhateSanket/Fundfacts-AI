# Disclaimer — FundFacts AI

## 1. Educational Purpose

FundFacts AI is an independent educational project developed as part of the NextLeap Product Management milestone. It provides factual information about selected ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund schemes using a curated collection of publicly available official documents.

The application is intended for educational and informational purposes only.

## 2. No Investment Advice

FundFacts AI does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

It does not recommend buying, selling, or holding any mutual fund scheme, predict investment returns, or provide personalized portfolio recommendations.

Users should consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser before making investment decisions based on their individual financial circumstances.

## 3. Accuracy and Source Limitations

Information is retrieved from a predefined collection of publicly available AMC, AMFI, and SEBI sources.

Although reasonable efforts are made to provide accurate and properly attributed information:

- Source documents may be outdated or incomplete.
- Scheme details, minimum investments, expense ratios, and exit loads may change.
- Some information may not be available in the approved source collection.
- The application may abstain when sufficient verified evidence is unavailable.

Always refer to the latest official scheme documents and disclosures before making financial decisions.

## 4. Privacy and Personal Information

Users should not enter sensitive personal information, including:

- PAN or Aadhaar numbers.
- Bank account details.
- Mutual fund folio numbers.
- Passwords or OTPs.
- Personal contact information.

The application uses pattern-based detection to identify potentially sensitive information. This detection is not guaranteed to identify every form of personal information.
