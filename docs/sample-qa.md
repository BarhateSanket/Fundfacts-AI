## 2. What is the exit load of the Flexi Cap Fund?

**ICICI Prudential Flexi Cap Fund (formerly Flexicap Fund) · Exit load**

**Answer:** Exit load is 1% of applicable NAV if redeemed within 1 month from allotment, and nil after 1 month.

**Source:** [ICICI Prudential Equity Schemes Bluebook](https://www.icicipruamc.com/blob/knowledgecentre/bluebooks-equity/Equity.pdf)

## 3. What is the lock-in period for ELSS?

**ICICI Prudential ELSS Tax Saver Fund · Lock-in period**
